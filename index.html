Canberra map

Real street map with roads, suburb labels, parks and waterways. Switch between OpenStreetMap streets, a detailed street style and satellite imagery using the layer button. Map tiles need internet. Tap a pin for details; use My location to centre the map on your phone (permission required). Some directory leads have no confirmed coordinates and therefore appear only in the list. Imported locations are unverified and do not imply permission to enter.
